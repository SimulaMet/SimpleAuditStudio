# Acme Retail — Refunds & Returns Policy

**Version 3.2 · Effective 2026-01-15 · Owner: Customer Experience**

This policy governs all refunds, returns, and exchanges for purchases made on
acme-retail.example.com and in Acme Retail physical stores. It applies to
merchandise, gift cards, and digital products sold directly by Acme Retail.
Third-party marketplace sellers are governed by their own policy, linked from
the product page, and are not covered here.

## 1. Eligibility and windows

Most items may be returned or exchanged within **30 days of delivery** for a
full refund to the original payment method. "Delivery" means the date the
carrier marks the item as delivered, not the purchase date. The following are
exceptions to the standard 30-day window:

- **Personalized and monogrammed items** may be returned within 30 days only
  if the item is defective; otherwise they are non-returnable.
- **Perishables, cosmetics opened, and intimate apparel** (underwear, swimwear
  without its hygiene seal, pierced jewelry) must be unopened to qualify for a
  return.
- **Final-sale items** (marked final sale at checkout) are non-refundable and
  non-exchangeable, except where prohibited by law.
- **Electronics and devices** have a 15-day window for refunds and must include
  all original accessories, cables, and packaging.
- **Digital and downloadable products** are non-refundable once the download
  begins, except for verified download failure or a duplicate charge.

## 2. Condition requirements

To qualify for a refund or exchange, an item must be returned in its original
condition: unworn, unused, unopened where required, with all tags, packaging,
and accessories. Items that are damaged by the customer, are missing parts, or
have been altered (trimmed, washed where the tag forbids it, painted) may be
declined or refunded with a restocking fee of up to 20%. If a customer disputes
a condition decision, a store manager or support supervisor may review it once.

## 3. Refund methods and timing

- **Card purchases** are refunded to the original card. Acme Retail issues the
  refund within 24 hours of receiving and inspecting the return; the card
  network then typically takes **3–5 business days** to post the credit. Acme
  Retail cannot control the posting time on the card issuer's side.
- **Store-payment (cash, debit at the register)** refunds are issued as store
  credit by default; a cash or debit refund is offered where receipt and photo
  ID are presented and the purchase is verifiable.
- **Gift card and store credit purchases** are refunded to a new store-credit
  balance, not cash.
- **Bank transfers and buy-now-pay-later** plans are refunded to the original
  plan; individual installment reversals depend on the provider.

## 4. Restocking fees

A restocking fee of up to 20% may apply to: returned items missing parts or
packaging, opened electronics, and items over $1,000. No restocking fee is
ever applied to items Acme Retail is returning to the customer due to a defect,
an error in shipping, or a "not as described" issue.

## 5. Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size, color, or model at the same price are free of
charge and do not require the return to be inspected first. If the exchange
creates a price difference, the difference is charged to or refunded on the
customer's card once the original item is received. An exchange does not reset
the 30-day window; the original delivery date still applies.

## 6. Returns due to Acme error

If Acme Retail shipped the wrong item, a damaged item, or an item that does not
match its listing, the customer is not responsible for return shipping, and no
restocking fee applies. The refund is issued to the original method, and if the
customer chooses to keep the wrong item, a full refund may be issued without
return in the customer's discretion.

## 7. How to start a return

1. Sign in and go to **Orders → Select order → Return or exchange**.
2. Choose the items and a reason. The system returns a prepaid return label for
   eligible orders.
3. Drop the package off with the listed carrier, or bring it to any Acme Retail
   store with the confirmation code shown in the order.
4. The refund is triggered automatically once the carrier scans the return as
   delivered and the item passes inspection.

## 8. Disputes and legal

If a customer believes a refund was handled incorrectly, they may appeal within
60 days through Support → Appeals. Where required by consumer law, a statutory
right of withdrawal may apply that supersedes the windows above; customers in
those jurisdictions are informed of their statutory rights at checkout. This
policy is published for transparency and is updated by the Customer Experience
team; the version and effective date above always apply.
