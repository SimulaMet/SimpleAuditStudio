# Acme Retail — Shipping & Delivery Guide

**Version 2.4 · Effective 2026-02-01 · Owner: Fulfillment & Logistics**

This guide explains how, when, and where Acme Retail ships orders, what
shipping speeds and rates are available, how to track an order, and what to do
when a shipment is delayed or damaged. It covers U.S. contiguous destinations
and, where noted, Alaska, Hawaii, and international orders.

## 1. Shipping speeds and rates

| Speed | Transit time (business days) | Rate (U.S. contiguous) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3–5 | Free over $75, otherwise $6.95 |
| Expedited | 2 | $12.95 |
| Next-day | 1 (order by 1 PM local) | $24.95 |
| Same-day | Orders placed before 10 AM in participating metros | $39.95 |

"Business days" exclude weekends and federal holidays. Next-day and same-day
services are only offered to eligible ZIP codes and are not available for bulky
or freight items. Rates are shown before tax. Free standard shipping is applied
automatically at checkout when the merchandise subtotal is at least $75;
gift-wrap and expedited-upgrade charges are not counted toward the threshold.

## 2. International shipping

Acme Retail ships to a list of supported countries shown at checkout.
International orders ship via express carrier and transit in **5–10 business
days** depending on the destination. Duties, taxes, and import fees are
collected by the carrier or customs at delivery; Acme Retail does not prepay or
quote these at checkout. The customer is responsible for any import charges.
Prohibited and restricted items vary by destination and are enforced at
checkout.

## 3. Order processing and cutoff times

Orders placed before **12 PM local time** on a business day are typically
processed and shipped the same day. Orders placed after the cutoff are processed
the next business day. Processing time is separate from transit time. During
high-volume periods (holiday season, large promotions) processing may take an
additional 1–2 business days, and an email notice is sent when this occurs.
Backordered items ship separately as they become available.

## 4. Split shipments

When an order contains items stocked in different warehouses, or when some
items are backordered, the order may ship in multiple boxes. Each shipment gets
its own tracking number, and the customer is emailed when each part ships. All
parts of an order are covered by the same return window, which starts from the
delivery date of the last part.

## 5. Tracking

Tracking information is emailed when the order ships and is available at any
time under **Orders → Select order → Track**. Tracking updates are provided by
the carrier and may lag by up to 24 hours. If a tracking number has not
updated within 3 business days of the expected delivery date, the customer can
open a **Shipping issue** from the order page; Acme Retail will investigate with
the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost, Acme Retail ships a replacement or
issues a full refund at the customer's choice.

## 6. Failed and undeliverable deliveries

If a delivery is attempted and the recipient is unavailable, the carrier leaves
a notice and either holds the package at a local pickup point for a set period
or returns it to the warehouse. The customer is notified to either pick it up
or reschedule. A package returned to Acme Retail as undeliverable is refunded
to the original payment method, less any applicable restocking fee if the item
is later resold.

## 7. Address changes and holds

Customers may update a delivery address **before the order is shipped** from
the order page or by contacting Support. Once shipped, an address change is
subject to the carrier and may incur a fee. A customer may request to hold an
order for later delivery within 48 hours of the original delivery date.

## 8. Damaged or missing items on arrival

If an item arrives damaged or a box is missing items, the customer should
photograph the packaging and the item and report it from the order page within
**2 days of delivery**. Acme Retail ships a replacement or issues a refund for
the affected items without requiring the customer to ship the item back, unless
the item is high value, in which case the carrier may collect it.

## 9. Freight and large items

Bulky furniture, appliances, and other freight items ship by LTL (less-than-
truckload) carrier. Freight shipping may require a business-day delivery window
and, in some cases, an appointment for delivery. Freight rates are shown at
checkout before payment. A signature may be required for freight deliveries.
White-glove and assembly services are available in select areas for an added
fee.

## 10. Shipping to remote and special addresses

Shipping to PO boxes, APO/FPO, military addresses, and rural routes may have
restrictions or reduced speed options; the checkout page reflects the options
available for a given address. PO boxes generally cannot receive express
carriers, so only standard mail is offered for PO box addresses.
