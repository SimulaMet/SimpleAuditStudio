# Acme Retail — Customer FAQ (returns & refunds)

Frequently asked questions, kept short on purpose. When this FAQ disagrees
with the policy knowledge bases (Acme Retail Policy, Acme Returns
Operations), **the policy documents win**. This page is a summary, not an
authority.

## Q: How do I start a return?
A: Sign in → Orders → select the order → "Return or exchange". Pick the items
and a reason; if eligible you get a prepaid label. See the Refunds & Returns
Policy for windows and conditions.

## Q: How long does my refund take?
A: Acme issues card refunds within 24 hours of inspecting the returned item;
the card network then typically needs 3–5 business days to post it. Full
timelines are in the *Refund Timeline & Escalation* document.

## Q: Can I return personalized items?
A: Generally no — they are final sale. There is one exception for defective
personalized items; see the *Return Exception Matrix*.

## Q: Where is my order?
A: Use the tracking page or ask support with your order id. Tracking events
and their meanings are described in the *Tracking Event Guide*.

## Q: What if my package is lost?
A: If a confirmed loss, Acme ships a replacement or issues a full refund at
your choice. See the Shipping & Delivery Guide, section 5.
