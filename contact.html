SimpleAudit Studio

Connect your models. Design your audits.

SimpleAudit Studio works with any OpenAI-compatible endpoint — but wiring up a production chatbot, a fine-tuned model, or a bespoke evaluation scenario takes more than copy-paste. The team behind the project is happy to help you get there.

What we can help with

Connect your models & chatbots

Point SimpleAudit Studio at Ollama, vLLM, Together, Groq, or any OpenAI-compatible API — including your own deployed chatbots and fine-tuned models. We help with endpoint configuration, authentication, and verifying that responses are captured correctly.

Design scenarios for your domain

Generic benchmarks rarely capture what matters in your setting. We help design versioned scenario sets and judge profiles tailored to your domain — so audits measure the behavior you actually care about.

How to reach us
