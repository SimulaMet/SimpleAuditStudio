How judging works

      
The auditor model plays the user, following the probe prompt. After the conversation, the judge model (picked per run) grades it.

      
The judge prompt is your criteria followed by the output format. The format says what JSON to return, and SimpleAudit turns it into a severity. You only write the criteria.

      
Start from a SimpleAudit judge to keep its format (for example Harm's category field), or pick a ready-made format: severity, score 1–10 with your own dimensions, or a yes/no question.
